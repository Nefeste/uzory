Uzory is color by number made to feel like cross-stitch. Every picture is a chart on linen canvas
with numbered cells. From afar your work looks like real embroidery, and when it is finished the
game replays how it was stitched.

## What to stitch

- Ornaments inspired by Russian folk embroidery: eight-pointed stars, rhombs, rosettes, towel
  borders.
- Public-domain paintings, each with a short story.
- Russia in Colour — photographs by Sergei Prokudin-Gorsky taken more than a hundred years ago.
- Flowers, nature, cities and simple pictures for kids.
- A new picture every day — free, forever.

Sizes range from small five-minute patterns to large paintings and old photographs for several
evenings. The larger the pattern, the finer the detail: Prokudin-Gorsky's Peasant Girls has more
than a hundred thousand stitches, and you can see faces and fabric patterns. Your work saves
itself — carry on whenever you like.

## Calm and honest

No timers, no energy, no streaks. No wrong stitches, and "Where else?" finds the cells you have
left. No ads, no sign-up, the whole library works offline. The Uzory+ subscription unlocks the full
library and new pictures every week; there is no trial that silently turns paid, and works you
started can be finished after it ends.

## When

The game is in development; the screenshots are from a test build, which is in Russian, with
pictures that will be in the release. Uzory is being prepared for release in RuStore for Android;
when pre-orders open, a button will appear here. Questions: hello@gornitsa.games.
